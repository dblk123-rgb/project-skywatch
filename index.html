Our Mission

Project Skywatch aims to investigate unusual aerial observations through independent measurements, transparent methods, and scientific review. We follow evidence wherever it leads, including conventional explanations.

Our long-term vision is to build an accessible, volunteer-powered sky observation network that contributes to research and public awareness.

A Connected Observation Network

Observe

Volunteer stations may one day monitor the sky with cameras and sensors, documenting events with accurate timestamps and equipment information.

Compare

Multiple stations could help correlate observations, estimate trajectories, and distinguish apparent motion from measured movement.

Investigate

Interesting detections could be flagged for human review and assisted analysis, with original evidence preserved securely.

The automated observation network is a future goal, not an active monitoring service.

Research Without Preset Conclusions

We aim to examine historical cases, international public records, astronomy, atmospheric phenomena, and unexplained observations. Our goal is to document both explained and unresolved cases with equal transparency.
